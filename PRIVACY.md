# Privacy Policy — HDR Screenshot Syncer

_Last updated: 2026-08-12_

HDR Screenshot Syncer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The app does not gather any personal or usage information.
- **No network connections.** The app makes no network requests and sends nothing off your device. It has no accounts, telemetry, analytics, or advertising.
- **Local operation only.** The app reads your display's current HDR state through Windows APIs and reads/writes a single local Windows Snipping Tool setting (`IsHDRToneMappingEnabled`) so that Snipping Tool's HDR screenshot color corrector matches your display. These reads and writes stay entirely on your device.
- **Optional autostart.** If you enable "start at logon," the app adds a per-user startup entry on your device only.

Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing collected to access, correct, or delete.

**Contact:** [github.com/mangokingTW/HdrScreenshotSyncer/issues](https://github.com/mangokingTW/HdrScreenshotSyncer/issues)

# 隱私權原則 — HDR Screenshot Syncer

_最後更新:2026-08-12_

HDR Screenshot Syncer 不收集、儲存、傳輸或分享任何個人資料。

- **不收集資料。** 本應用程式不蒐集任何個人或使用資訊。
- **無網路連線。** 本應用程式不發出任何網路要求,也不會將任何內容傳出你的裝置。沒有帳號、遙測、分析或廣告。
- **僅本機運作。** 本應用程式透過 Windows API 讀取螢幕目前的 HDR 狀態,並讀寫單一項本機的 Windows 剪取工具設定(`IsHDRToneMappingEnabled`),讓剪取工具的 HDR 螢幕擷取色彩校正器與螢幕狀態一致。這些讀寫全部留在你的裝置上。
- **選用的開機自動啟動。** 若你啟用「開機時啟動」,本應用程式僅會在你的裝置上新增一個使用者層級的啟動項目。

由於沒有任何資料離開你的裝置,因此沒有可供存取、更正或刪除的已收集資料。

**聯絡方式:** [github.com/mangokingTW/HdrScreenshotSyncer/issues](https://github.com/mangokingTW/HdrScreenshotSyncer/issues)
